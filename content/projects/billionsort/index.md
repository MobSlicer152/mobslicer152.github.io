Someone dared me to visualize sorting a billion numbers, so I came up with this visualization
that I think is pretty neat. The "expected" X and Y of a number given that the list is sorted
determine the red and blue of the pixel, so you get a nice smooth gradient at the end.

[GitHub](https://github.com/MobSlicer152/billionsort)
